# HOW IT WORKS

To start I want to make it clear that this is a draft. It will most likely be a draft in perpetuity. 
We have a mission, and this is the primary purpose. Everything we do should be for the support of 
that purpose. This process (in this form and every form after it) will be periodically reviewed, 
assessed by the standards of the mission and it's place in the ever changing world around it, 
re-written as needed to better support the mission then implemented with a date set for the entire 
process to be completed again. The mission will always stay the same, but the means by which we 
carry this mission out may change as needed for the greater good of the mission.

Open IT is an idea and a vision. It is not a commercial product or the property of any individual. 
It will not be owned or dependent on any one individual, including myself the writer of this 
document. Since the goal of Open IT is that everyone have access to the help and tools they need
then it would be counter to the mission to restrict anyone from adopting the idea and putting it 
into practice also.

NeighborhoodNerd21 acts only as a centralized point of contact to allow organizations to reach 
help. There are two sides to this organism. There is the functional side made up of volunteers 
willing to help and there is the service side comprised of non-tech focused organizations that 
reach out for assistance. NN21 will act as a port of connection between the two holding 
communications to professional standards and will eventually be accountable to a board of sorts. 
My hope is that there will be collaboration and a structure of mutuality in any decisions or 
proposed solutions before it crosses this port.
