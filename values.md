# CORE VALUES

The strength of Open IT is rooted in three core values: 

- community
- opportunity
- integrity

## Community

One of the many great things about Open Source Software, are the communities that grow 
around and support each project. Users and developers are able to communicate without 
proprietary gate-keeping. For Open IT this means real connection with organizations. 
There isn't the abstraction of support ticketing and service requests. Real contact on 
an equal plane with professionals who value awareness and remediation instead of 
"diagnose, prescribe execute and bill". We do not want you to depend on us. We want to 
help you learn to confidently depend on yourselves.

## Opportunity

Another benefit of FOSS is the opportunities it creates. Individuals who would 
otherwise be unable to access tools for image or video editing, desktop publishing, and 
programming have been able to learn new skills and passions through the availability of 
free and open source alternatives to costly proprietary applications that had become 
industry standards. The Linux kernel, created by Linus Torvalds, was developed to 
provide a free Unix-like operating system that would allow anyone who wanted to learn to 
use a Unix system to do so without the expensive commercial Unix licenses. Open IT will 
promote the use of these same projects to allow organizations the ability to effectively 
perform in their fields without needing to lot large portions of grants that are already 
stretching thin.

## Integrity

FOSS inherently allows a projects developers to be accountable to it's users. No hidden 
agendas, and secret back-doors or user tracking. Everything is out in the open and this 
transparency works on levels no proprietary commercial project will ever reach. This 
same openness is applied in Open IT. Everything we do, everyone we are, anything we 
generate will be shareable and free to review. Openness where it counts, privacy for the 
organization where it's needed.
